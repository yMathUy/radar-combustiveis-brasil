About the data

Values represent prices observed at fuel stations surveyed by ANP. They do not necessarily represent every fuel station operating in each state.

Partial weeks are excluded from the main analyses. Liquid fuels are measured per liter, while compressed natural gas is measured per cubic meter.

Source: Brazilian National Agency of Petroleum, Natural Gas and Biofuels — ANP.
